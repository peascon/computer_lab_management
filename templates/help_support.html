🚀 Getting Started

1. First Steps - Login & Dashboard

When you first access the Lab Management System, you'll see the login page. Use your credentials to access the dashboard.

  • ✓ Enter your username and password
  • ✓ Click "Sign In"
  • ✓ You'll be directed to the main dashboard
2. Understanding the Dashboard

The dashboard gives you a quick overview of the entire system:

  • ✓ Active Loans: Number of equipment currently on loan
  • ✓ Pending Repairs: Equipment waiting for maintenance
  • ✓ Quick access cards for Students, Teachers, Labs, and Inventory
3. Navigation Menu

The top navigation bar contains links to all major sections:

  • ✓ Dashboard: Home screen with overview
  • ✓ Students: Manage student records
  • ✓ Teachers: Manage faculty/staff
  • ✓ Labs: Manage laboratory spaces
  • ✓ Inventory: Manage lab materials
  • ✓ Activity Log: View all system activities
  • ✓ Maintenance: Track equipment repairs

⭐ Features Guide

Student Management

Keep track of all students using the lab facilities.

How to register a student:
1. Click "Students" in the menu
2. Click "Register New Student"
3. Fill in name, department, and email
4. Click "Save Student Record"
Equipment Loans & Returns

Track when equipment leaves and returns from the lab.

How to loan equipment:
1. Go to "Activity Log"
2. Click "Log New Event / Loan"
3. Select person and "Lend Out Lab Material"
4. Choose the equipment and click "Process Entry"
Maintenance Tracking

Report broken or malfunctioning equipment.

How to report maintenance:
1. Click "Maintenance" in the menu
2. Click "Report Broken Equipment"
3. Select the equipment and describe the issue
4. Click "File Maintenance Ticket"

🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues: Below are solutions to frequently encountered problems.
Login Issues

Problem: Can't log in

  • Verify your username is correct
  • Check if Caps Lock is on
  • Use "Forgot Password" if needed
  • Contact admin if issue persists
Page Not Loading

Problem: Pages won't load or are slow

  • Clear your browser cache
  • Try a different browser
  • Check your internet connection
  • Refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R)
Data Not Saving

Problem: Forms don't save

  • Check for validation errors (red text)
  • Verify all required fields are filled
  • Try again after a few seconds
  • Contact support if problem continues
